# Provenance and semantic boundary

Every kind in `DECLARED_KINDS` rests on a statement by Tyler Roost. The
statements are quoted below. The executable frame, the two-relation
discipline, and the tests are an assistant-proposed [FRAME]. They are not
his words.

## 2026-09-21: areality, surreality, preality, base-reality

USER-STATED, quoted from the declaration as preserved in Hyperethics layer
L3, with elisions marked:

> "…Thought is seen as an amorphous objectification of a projection of a
> reality one can construct with their mind. In my own taxonomy of reality
> types this would be areality, as it is an abstract artificial reality. I
> also separate surreality which is the dream reality, preality (possibility
> reality, … N-d universal base reality …"

The same layer records the discipline this field keeps: the four kinds are
pairwise distinct, with no order, ranking, nesting, or degree, and the list
is not closed.

**Source state.** The declaring file is `L3_consciousness.hm` in
[Hyperethics](https://github.com/TimeLordRaps/hyperethics). At the time of
writing it is staged locally and in no commit, so no commit pin exists. The
inspected bytes had git blob `881e72acc6166c4a4f1db65bd78492a0416727ff` and
SHA-256 `55451002e0f6bdae51f6697c496b1a5345c5c518b5984f5d3e238e739fb4065e`.
The elided parts of the quotation are left out on purpose. They carry
personal material that has not been published.

## 2026-09-23: oreality

USER-STATED, preserved at
[metamathethicology `846ce08`](https://github.com/TimeLordRaps/metamathethicology/blob/846ce08de66f452d3942eea3d5aedbd8a03bbf47/oreality.hm):

> "…we already should have Areality (Abstraction reality where abstract
> thoughts exist), Oreality springs out from Areality (might actually use
> spring like hyperphysical equations), then also Preality (Possibility
> reality) [holds 5+dimensional objects like the block multiverse]. Our
> reality is the 4D projection of possibility reality through our coherent
> projections of Areality hyperprobabilistically through Oreality."

This field takes the kind oreality from that statement. The
hyperprobabilistic projection stays in metamathethicology, where it is a
transport between two fields.

[OPEN] The gloss of areality differs between the two statements:
"abstract artificial reality" (09-21) and "Abstraction reality where
abstract thoughts exist" (09-23). They are recorded side by side, and
neither overrides the other.

## 2026-09-26: sempiternality

USER-STATED: sempiternality is "always existing outside eternity". Reality,
surreality, preality, and areality integrate compositionally into it, and it
is simultaneously their abstract class and their container class. This is
quoted from a Hyperstratum working draft that is untracked. Its inspected
bytes had SHA-256
`a3c3efc20d27289670e1104529a0c60e5b6ccf567924d71eb0da72f8cda01f8a`.

bunch of base realities in a grid…". Hyperspace owns that criterion, and

## 2026-09-29: a sempiternality inside a base-reality

USER-STATED, excerpted:

> "…the environment/base-reality needs to expand to be able to obtain a
> sempiternality inside of a base-reality, sempiternality base-realities
> (those filled with sufficiently expanding light-cones of prealities and
